Using the model

Drag to move around, scroll or pinch to zoom, and move the year slider in the model’s panel to watch the palace change. Select a building to read where its position, date and appearance come from.

With a keyboard, press Tab to move into the model. Its panel has buttons for each view and for zooming and turning, and a list of the buildings standing in the chosen year.
