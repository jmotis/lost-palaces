The three timelines below show Tudor and Stuart residences over time. An arrow at the left of a bar means it began before 1400; an arrow at the right means it continues after 1800. The lost residences with a [3D model](#tl-models) are in green, the lost [residences without a model](#tl-unmodeled) are in white, and the residences that [are still habitable today](#tl-extant) are in golden brown.
